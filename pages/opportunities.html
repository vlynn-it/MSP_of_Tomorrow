INTAKE FORM
Customized form for submitting tickets.

Target = Internal and Client Facing
Current method:
Phone, email, desktop icon
Issues:
Inconsistent data, duplicate contacts, missing information
Improvements:
Streamline ticket creation, increase triage effiency, 1st step toward automatic AI triage
LICENSING AUDITING
Automated syncing of seat counts to agreements.

Target = Internal and Client Facing
Current method:
90% manual effort, 10% automation
Issues:
Long hours, incorrect quantities, billing inaccuracies
Improvements:
Billing accuracy and transparency, time savings
SELF SERVICE PORTAL
Single web interface to unite important tools and information.

Target = Internal and Client Facing
Current method:
Multiple consoles internally, CW PSA portal for clients
Issues:
Console fatigue, limited customer facing abilities
Improvements:
Custom dashboards, automated outcomes, no-wait service
AI TRIAGE
AI automation for ticket cleanup and cataegorization.

Target = Internal
Current method:
Triage by PSA workflows, dispatcher, and RRT.
Issues:
Typos, incorrect assignments,
> 1 issue/ticket
Improvements:
Standarized types/subtypes, suggested solutions, automatic scheduling
MONTHLY REVIEW & QBR REPORTS
Dynamic reports containing summary graphs and information.

Target = Internal and Client Facing
Current method:
VCIOs and TAMs use many tools to collect info
Issues:
Time consuming, short lived, missed highlights
Improvements:
Increase client visibility, decrease administrative effort
SLA IMPROVEMENTS
AI evaluation of SOWs and time entries.

Target = Internal
Current method:
Job time estimates, manual and periodic review
Issues:
Missed time savings, inaccurate estimates
Improvements:
Automatic flagging of inconsistent entries
